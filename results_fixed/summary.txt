KNOWN LIMITATIONS (important disclosures)
============================================================

1. SURVIVORSHIP BIAS (High severity)
   Universe = current Nifty 50 constituents only.
   Stocks removed from Nifty 50 due to poor performance
   are absent from training data. Backtest returns are
   likely overstated as a result.

2. LOOK-AHEAD BIAS IN FUNDAMENTALS (Medium-High severity)
   Fundamental features (PE, profit_margin, etc.) are
   point-in-time snapshots, not timestamped quarterly data.
   Quarterly earnings are published 45-60 days after quarter
   end. Model may have 'seen' data not available at trade time.

3. BACKTEST OVERFITTING (High severity)
   Feature selection, window length, and portfolio size were
   all chosen by observing this dataset. Forward performance
   will likely be lower than backtest results.

4. TRANSACTION COSTS (Modelled — 0.3% round-trip)
   Brokerage + STT + exchange + impact cost approximated.
   Actual costs may vary by broker and stock liquidity.

5. SMALL UNIVERSE (Low-Medium severity)
   48 stocks is a small cross-sectional universe.
   Planned expansion to Nifty 100 in V4.1.
